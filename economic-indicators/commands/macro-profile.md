---

Load the `country-macro-profile` skill to build a macro snapshot: growth,
inflation, labor, external balance, policy stance and fiscal position, each with
its trend, plus the cycle read and the main risks.

Date every figure and name the source per block. If no country was given, ask
which one and what the profile is for — the emphasis differs between a research
note, a cross-border deal, and an allocation decision.
