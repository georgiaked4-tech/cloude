Load the `central-bank-watch` skill.

Build or refresh the meeting grid for the banks in scope — next meeting, current
rate, last move, and what is priced — then summarize the stance and where the
market-implied path diverges from guidance.

Take meeting dates from each bank's own site rather than a calendar aggregator.
Default to the Fed, ECB, BoE, BoJ and BoC when no banks are named.
