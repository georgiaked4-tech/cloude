Load the `week-ahead-macro` skill to write the forward-looking macro note.

Rank the coming week's events by potential to move prices, not by importance
category, and cover the top three in depth: what is priced, what would count as a
surprise with specific thresholds, and the asset-level implication. Everything
else goes in a compact table.

Default to a global note for a generalist audience unless the arguments say
otherwise. Keep it to one page.
